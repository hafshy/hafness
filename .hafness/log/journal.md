# Journal

Lessons from failed or badly-passing runs. A lesson that generalizes gets promoted:
to `rules/` if it is a judgement call, to `rubric.md` if it is a quality bar, to
`verify.sh` if it is mechanically checkable, to `policy.md` if it must never happen.

Format:

    ## YYYY-MM-DD — <lesson in one line>
    Run: <evidence id>
    What happened:
    Why the harness allowed it:
    Promoted to: rules/ | rubric | verify.sh | policy | not promoted (one-off)
